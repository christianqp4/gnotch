## Per-app sound mute (sub-chat of the day chat) · 00:17–01:20 (6 Nov)

**What was done**
- Why: a hidden browser-pane test tab kept playing video sound. Chris muted the page he could see, but the sound kept going. He wants Windows-style per-app mute: keep sound on as a signal, and silence only one source.
- Research (notes: `~/Library/Application Support/Claude/side-session-notes/local_a1731c9e-…/per-app-mute-plan-2026-11-06.md`): Core Audio lists every process using sound (`kAudioHardwarePropertyProcessObjectList`, `IsRunningOutput`). Reading the list needs no permission. Muting one app is a process tap with `muteBehavior = .muted` (macOS 14.2+). No driver needed. SoundSource costs about $39–49.
- Finding: the Claude app plays sound through its own helper, `Claude Helper` (`audio.mojom.AudioService`). That helper is its own Core Audio process, so Claude can be muted alone, but only all of its browser-pane tabs at once. macOS sees processes, not tabs.
- Built in GNotch on branch `sound-mixer` (worktree `.claude/worktrees/sound-mixer`), commit `5629f98`, from `b2bad0f`. Not merged and not installed.
  - New `SoundMixer.swift`.
  - Top band: a speaker left of the cup, with a count of apps playing. Clicking it opens a list with a mute per app.
  - Red indicators while anything is muted: a red `speaker.slash` in the band, and the menu bar icon turns red with "Unmute …" items.
  - Quitting GNotch unmutes everything.
  - `Info.plist` gets `NSAudioCaptureUsageDescription`.
  - Dev check: `GNOTCH_SOUND_CHECK=1`. Snapshots 26 and 27.

**Decisions**
- It lives in GNotch, not in a new app. Chris: "Yes, in GNotch."
- Mutes are kept only in memory, so a mute can't be forgotten across restarts.

**Checked / not checked**
- Checked without sound: the build is clean, the live list groups helpers under their app (Claude, Safari, Brave), and the snapshots look right.
- Not tried yet: the mute itself, whether a permission prompt appears (GNotch is ad-hoc signed, so a prompt may come back after rebuilds), unmute on quit, and the menu bar icon in real use.
